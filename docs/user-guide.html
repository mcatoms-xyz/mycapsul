Welcome to Your Capsul

Getting Started

What You Just Got

Four pieces, all on your machine.

🤖

Your Capsul Agent

Claude (Anthropic's AI) running through Cowork (Anthropic's desktop environment). MyCapsul has no AI of its own; it gives Claude a framework to work as your agent, with your context, on your machine.

🖥️

The Dashboard

A single HTML file showing your schedule, budget, tasks, countdowns, and priorities. Runs in your browser, stores data locally, and your agent can update it.

🌱

Your Seed

Local plain-text files (mostly markdown) holding everything about your life — identity, finances, schedule, health, career, goals. You and your agent both read and write them.

📥

The Inbox

A drop folder, _inbox/, inside your MyCapsul directory. Put anything in it — a bank statement, a forwarded email, a note to yourself. Your agent reads it, routes it to the right Seed file, and tells you what it did.

First Steps

Your First Session

What happens the first time.

The Onboarding Wizard

The first time you open the dashboard, a 5-step wizard appears. About 2 minutes; every step is skippable.

Step 1 — Welcome
Introduces MyCapsul and links to the docs.
Step 2 — You + Your Style
Your name, dashboard name, agent name. One of 7 palettes. Your weekly schedule type (same every week, variable/co-parenting, rotating shifts).
Step 3 — People, Nodes & Cubes
Important people (family, partners, kids). Your life areas (career, health, financial, creative) — these become the Nodes your projects live in.
Step 4 — Your Money
What to track (daily budget, bills, subscriptions, debt, savings) and your income type (salaried, freelance, hourly). Your agent fills in the numbers in your first session.
Step 5 — Your Capsul is Ready
A summary of what you configured and which widgets are active. Hit "Launch Your Capsul."
Skipping is fine. Your agent brings up anything you skipped in your first conversation.

Your First Agent Session

After the wizard, start a Cowork session.

1

Your agent boots up

It reads your Seed, checks the inbox, pulls your schedule, and loads last session's handoff. On the first session there's no history, so it introduces itself and picks up from the wizard.

2

You tell it about your life

Your job, your family, your schedule, your bills, your goals. Your agent files it into the right places; you don't need to know the file structure.

3

Your dashboard comes alive

As it learns, it populates the dashboard: schedule blocks, countdowns, budget numbers, Focus 3. Blank to useful in one conversation.

4

Session wraps up

Your agent writes a handoff — what it learned, what's pending. Next session picks up from there. No re-explaining.

The Foundation

How the Seed Works

The Seed is how MyCapsul knows you: a folder of plain-text files, one per topic, readable by you and the AI alike.

📁 seed/
  index.md ← The router. Maps topics to files.
  identity.md ← Who you are, your family, key relationships
  finances.md ← Budget, bills, debts, income, goals
  schedule.md ← Events, deadlines, recurring patterns, time templates
  career.md ← Job details, income, professional goals
  health.md ← Medical info, wellness notes, conditions
  changelog.md ← Running log of every update, timestamped
  ...and more as your life grows
schedule.md
# Schedule ## Recurring Patterns - Weekdays: Work 9am-5pm, gym 6:30am (M/W/F) - Fridays: Game night when available ## Upcoming - Mar 14: Dentist appointment, 2:30pm - Mar 21: Quarterly review at work - Apr 1: Rent due
📝
You can edit these yourself — they're markdown. Mostly you won't need to: mention a deadline and your agent writes it to schedule.md; mention a doctor visit and it goes in health.md.

The Router (index.md)

Your agent doesn't read every Seed file every time. The router maps topics to files: money opens finances.md, a date opens schedule.md.

The Changelog

Every Seed update is logged — what, when, why. A running history you can read back.

Working Together

Talking to Your Agent

Your Capsul Agent is Claude, running through Cowork, guided by the MyCapsul framework. Less a search engine, more a chief of staff who's read the file.

Just talk normally

No special commands. "I have a dentist appointment next Tuesday at 2:30" updates your schedule, checks for conflicts, and logs the change. "How much do I owe on my credit card?" pulls from finances.md.

It updates things in real time

A new bill, a schedule change, a health note — your agent writes it to the right Seed file as you say it, not at the end.

It'll ask before it acts

It presents options; it doesn't decide for you. Budget calls, scheduling conflicts, anything involving money or family — it flags and asks.

It flags things before you ask

Deadlines, bills due, schedule conflicts — boot includes a scan for what matters today, so you don't have to remember to check.

⚡
Good to know: your agent can't send email, buy anything, or contact anyone without explicit permission. It drafts and recommends; you pull the trigger.

Controls

Settings & Features

What's in the header and the widgets.

⚙️

Settings Drawer

The gear icon in the header. Change palette (7: MyCapsul default, Ocean, Sunset, Forest, Lavender, Amber, Slate), show/hide widgets, drag to reorder, manage the agent sync connection.

📐

Widget Resize & Reorder

Each widget header has a resize button: compact, standard, wide, or full-width. Reorder by dragging in the Settings drawer, or tell your agent: "Move the budget widget above the calendar."

ℹ️

Widget Info

Every widget has an ℹ️ button: what it does, how to use it, things to tell your agent.

🕐

Ring Clock Editing

Click the Ring to edit any day's blocks. Each block's category (work, build, family, admin, personal, break) sets its color. Your agent generates them each session; you can edit by hand.

📅

Calendar Popover

Click a day for its events and a mini Ring preview; pin it to keep it open while you navigate. In co-parent mode, custody weeks get a background tint. US holidays appear automatically.

🔮

TimeCapsul (Month + Year)

Pin a month on the Timeline widget: Month opens that month as its own TimeCapsul, Year opens the year-in-review with one section per month. Both are built from your highlights, goal progress, journal excerpts and finished cubes, plus the summary your agent writes for each month (the tcAgent_YYYY key, documented in the agent skill) at the first session after the 1st. Save the Year page before December ends — it keys off the current calendar year.

How It Compounds

The Session Loop

Every session follows the same loop: boot, work, wrap, hand off.

🔄 Boot Reads the Seed, checks the inbox, loads the handoff, surfaces what matters today →
⚡ Work New info is filed to the Seed as you talk. Dashboard updated. →
📝 Wrap Updates Seed modules, syncs the dashboard, logs changes →
📋 Handoff Writes a short summary for next session — what happened, what's pending

This is how MyCapsul learns without a database.

No model is trained on you. Your agent reads files, updates files, and writes a handoff; the next one reads the handoff. Cumulative knowledge in structured text you can read every word of.

Zero Friction

Using the Inbox

A folder called _inbox/ inside MyCapsul/, created at setup. Drop things in it.

1

Drop a file in the _inbox/ folder

A bank CSV, a school calendar PDF, a phone note, a forwarded email — anything.

2

Your agent picks it up

At the start of every session, or when you say "check the inbox," it reads everything new and works out where it belongs.

3

It routes and reports

A bill goes to finances.md. A school event goes to schedule.md. A bank statement gets its spending data extracted. Your agent tells you what it did with each file.

Extensibility

Make It Yours

Everything is markdown, JSON, and HTML. No plugin system to learn, no proprietary format. The architecture is the extensibility.

Your Capsul grows the way your life does.

The Seed pattern works for anything: add a markdown file, tell your agent about it, and that part of your life is in the system. The structure that tracks your finances can track your fitness, a side business, or your kid's soccer schedule.

1

Add new Seed modules

Want to track something new? Just say so. "Start tracking my photography gear and shoots." Your agent creates the file and adds it to the router.

2

Reshape the dashboard

A new card, a different layout, a tracker for something specific — tell your agent and it writes the change. Or open the file in any code editor; it's HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

3

Define your own protocols

The operating manual is a markdown file your agent reads every session. Want it to check a particular inbox each morning, or run a workflow when you say a trigger phrase? Add it to the protocols.

4

Build entirely new systems

A music producer could add a Seed module for projects, a dashboard tab for session logs, and protocols for mix notes. A freelancer could build invoicing and client tracking. Same patterns as the core system.

Open by Design

Your data files

Markdown and JSON. Open, edit, delete in any text editor.

Your dashboard

One HTML file. Views in any browser; automatic sync needs desktop Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc — elsewhere the sync pill becomes a one-tap export. No build tools.

Your operating manual

A markdown file defining how your agent behaves. It reads it fresh every session.

Your session history

Handoff files and changelogs, in plain text.

No proprietary formats. Nothing to export — it's already usable anywhere.

💡
You don't need to be technical. Most customization is conversation. If you are, nothing is hidden or compiled.
Your Data

Where Your Data Lives

Everything is on your machine.

In your first Cowork session you pick a folder. Your agent creates a MyCapsul/ directory inside it holding everything: dashboard, Seed, inbox, widgets, agent config.

MyCapsul is the framework that makes Claude your Capsul Agent.

When your agent needs to think, it sends only the context for the current task over Anthropic's encrypted API and writes the result back to your local files. Your full dataset never leaves your machine. Nothing is stored in the cloud.

📁

You choose where it lives

Any parent folder — Documents, Desktop, wherever. MyCapsul creates MyCapsul/ inside it: seed/ for your knowledge base, _inbox/ for file drops, widgets/ for add-on widgets, and your dashboard HTML file.

📂

Your files are plain text

Markdown, JSON, HTML. Open, change, delete in any editor.

🔒

Keep secrets out of the Seed

Keep account numbers, passwords, and other sensitive identifiers out of your seed files. Anything your agent reads is sent to Anthropic during a session, like any Claude conversation — there is no separate protected store.

💾

Back up by copying

Copy the MyCapsul/ folder. Everything is files — dashboard, Seed, widgets, agent config.
